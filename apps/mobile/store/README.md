# App Store screenshots

The uploadable sets for the current release.

- `screenshots/ios-6.9/`: iPhone 6.9", 1320 by 2868, from iPhone 17 Pro captures. App Store Connect scales these down for the smaller iPhones.
- `screenshots/iphone-duo/`: iPhone Duo inner screen, 2853 by 2007, flat and folded a little in Apple's Duo frame.

Each tile is the night ground with the badge wave, a cream headline over a violet second line, and the app in a device. They are drawn from simulator captures by an HTML page outside the repo and exported in headless Chrome; to change one, recapture the screen and re-export rather than editing the PNG.
